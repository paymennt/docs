---
title: Google Pay™ on hosted checkout
sidebar_label: Google Pay™
---

**Google Pay™** is shown automatically on the **paymennt** [Hosted Checkout](../3-hosted/index.md) payment page when it is enabled for your account. No integration or setup is needed on your side.

If Google Pay™ is not available on your checkout page, contact **paymennt** support.

:::warning Adhere to Google policies

All merchants are required to follow the [Google Pay and Wallet API's Acceptable Use Policy](https://payments.developers.google.com/terms/aup) and accept the terms defined in the [Google Pay Terms of Service](https://payments.developers.google.com/terms/sellertos).

:::

For Google Pay™ branding, see the [Google Pay™ Web brand guidelines](https://developers.google.com/pay/api/web/guides/brand-guidelines).
